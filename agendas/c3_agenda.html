Week 1 Class 3 Agenda

👤 Faculty Instructor

  • Stephen Metts | he/him
  • Email | sm6221@columbia.edu

Class Time | Location

  • Date: Wednesday 07/01/26
  • ⏰ Time: 11:10 a.m.–1:00 p.m. and 3:10–5:00 p.m.
  • Location: 140 URIS HALL
Important

Important: Class 3 builds directly from the Class 2 vantage point and urban sketching exercise. Students should bring their Class 2 sketch, recorded latitude/longitude coordinates, sketchbook, notes, and laptop to both sessions.

Class 3 Agenda Items - AM Session

11:10 - 11:20 am

  • Attendance and informal announcements.
  • Brief review of the day’s focus: from observation to mapping.

11:20 - 11:50 am

  • Assignment 2 Sharing: Vantage Point Sketches
    • Students share selected observation locations and sketches.
    • Discussion of what students chose to include and leave out.
    • Reflection on how vantage point, field of view, and orientation shaped each sketch.
    • Comparison of street-level observation, photography, sketching, and annotation.

11:50 am - 12:10 pm

  • Reading Discussion:
    • Jan Gehl: Life Between Buildings: Using Public Space.
    • Human-scale urban experience and the relationship between buildings, public space, and everyday activity.
    • Discussion of how mobile phones, digital maps, and media may change how people experience urban space.

12:10 - 12:35 pm

  • Class 3 Lecture: From Observation to Mapping
    • What is geographic space?
    • What is geographic scale?
    • How can we connect street-level observation to geographic space?
    • Latitude and longitude as location data.
    • Observation points as spatial data.
    • Street view versus map view.
    • Introduction to GIS and spatial layers.
    • Why urban designers use maps alongside sketches and field notes.

12:35 - 1:00 pm

  • Transition Workshop: Preparing for Class 3 Assignment
    • Reviewing coordinates from Class 2 fieldwork.
    • Understanding how a recorded vantage point becomes a mapped point.
    • Preparing laptops for QGIS installation and setup.
    • Introduction to QGIS, spatial points, and basemaps.
    • Assignment expectations, file organization, and troubleshooting.

Class 3 Agenda Items - PM Session

3:10 - 3:40 pm

  • Assignment 3 Introduction and QGIS Setup - Class 3 Assignment
    • Review of assignment goals and deliverables.
    • QGIS installation and startup.
    • Creating a new QGIS project.
    • Understanding the map canvas and layer panel.
    • Introduction to coordinate systems and spatial data.
    • Questions and setup support.

3:40 - 4:30 pm

  • Observation Point Mapping Exercise - Class 3 Assignment
    • Students use their Class 2 latitude/longitude coordinates.
    • Students create a spatial point representing their observation location.
    • Students add the observation point to QGIS.
    • Students install and use the QuickMapServices plugin.
    • Students explore basemaps relative to their observation point, including:
      • OpenStreetMap.
      • Satellite imagery.
      • Hybrid or labeled basemaps.
      • Other available contextual baselayers.
    • Students compare what is visible from the street with what is visible from the map.

4:30 - 4:50 pm - Mapping Discussion and Troubleshooting

Important

Important: Students should save their QGIS project files and exported map images before the end of class. We will use these materials as part of the Class 3 assignment submission and future mapping work.

  • Students review mapped observation points.
  • Discussion of street-level perspective versus map-based perspective.
  • Troubleshooting QGIS setup, basemap access, and coordinate entry.
  • Discussion of how scale changes what can be seen and understood.

4:50 - 5:00 pm

  • Reflection and Wrap-Up
    • What can maps reveal that field observation cannot?
    • What can field observation reveal that maps cannot?
    • How does mapping change your understanding of your observation point?
    • What questions do you now have about the larger urban context?

Week 1 Class 3 Task List

  1. Attend Week 1 | Class 3 Meeting (07/01/26) starting at 11:10 a.m.–1:00 p.m. and then resuming 3:10–5:00 p.m.
  2. Participate in the Assignment 2 sharing discussion and Gehl reading discussion.
  3. Review posted materials; ask instructor any remaining questions or areas of clarification.
  4. Conduct Class 3 Assignment across both sessions.
  5. Complete the day’s homework assignment located at the end of Class 3 Assignment.
